# Paper Exemplars

Use these papers as source references for data-figure patterns. They are selected because their
result figures, benchmark framing, or evaluation summaries are useful to inspect. Do not copy the
paper images into the skill unless the license and redistribution terms explicitly allow it.

When using one of these as a concrete example, open the source page/PDF and refer to the paper's
actual data-figure pattern. Do not invent a replacement example with made-up data or labels.

## Selection Criteria

- clear benchmark or evaluation summary
- clear ablation, trade-off, sensitivity, or distribution plot
- public PDF or open-access record
- useful across more than one venue family
- link-only unless a specific asset is separately cleared

## What to Borrow

- one clear evaluation panel family
- one concise trade-off, sensitivity, or failure panel
- one repeated color family per method or role
- one caption style that explains the scientific question, not the graphic itself
- benchmark/sample/metric context sufficient for the plot to stand alone

## What Not to Borrow

- published figures copied into the repo
- decorative shading
- low-contrast screenshots
- overly dense multi-hue legends
- panels that duplicate the same result in different forms
